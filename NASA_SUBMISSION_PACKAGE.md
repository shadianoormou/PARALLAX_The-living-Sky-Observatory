## Project name

PARALLAX X — The Living Sky Observatory

## Short mission description

PARALLAX X helps researchers and citizens triage possible moving or changing objects in repeated SPHEREx observations without confusing artifacts for discoveries. It registers observations, applies quality gates, compares residuals across epochs and bands, preserves null results, and produces a provenance-linked handoff for human review. The system is designed to make uncertainty visible: a candidate is evidence for follow-up, never an automatic discovery claim.

## NASA data source

NASA SPHEREx public archive products accessed through NASA/IPAC Infrared Science Archive (IRSA), including public FITS cutouts, WCS metadata, variance arrays, and quality flags. The project also includes a deterministic, explicitly labelled synthetic validation dataset for regression testing; synthetic records are never presented as NASA observations.

## Science validation

- Three independent SPHEREx sky fields can be checked through the live validation workflow.
- Same-target, multi-band evidence graphs expose per-band epochs, quality gates, candidates, null results, and provenance.
- Registration, differencing, candidate extraction, and screening are tested against deterministic injected moving, variable, low-SNR, and artifact cases.
- Real archive processing preserves WCS, variance, non-nominal flags, FITS provenance, retrieval time, and checksums.
- A `COMPARISON BLOCKED`, `SINGLE-BAND ONLY`, or null result remains a valid scientific outcome.
- Cross-band consistency is evidence context for review; it is not a discovery probability or confirmation.

## Video requirement and plan

The supplied submission form asks for a **240-second video**, which is **4 minutes maximum**. Record the final version at **3:45–3:55** to leave a safety margin. A 2-minute cut is valid but leaves less room for the science validation and provenance story.

### 3:50 demo outline

1. **00:00–00:25 — Problem:** repeated sky images can contain real changes and imaging artifacts.
2. **00:25–00:55 — Mission:** Observe → Compare → Reject artifacts → Cross-band verify → Human review.
3. **00:55–01:35 — Live demo:** open the Vercel observatory and run the guided judge brief.
4. **01:35–02:15 — Measurement:** show registered epochs, difference/residual view, displacement, SNR, and comparison quality.
5. **02:15–02:55 — Multi-band evidence:** show `MULTI-BAND CONSISTENT`, `SINGLE-BAND ONLY`, `COMPARISON BLOCKED`, or `NO PROMOTED RESIDUAL` outcomes.
6. **02:55–03:25 — Science validation:** show SPHEREx/IRSA provenance, WCS/flags/variance gates, and the synthetic-data disclosure.
7. **03:25–03:50 — Impact:** export/share the evidence bundle and state the next action: human follow-up, re-observation, or reject as artifact.

Do not say “Planet X discovered.” Say “candidate requiring additional verification.”
